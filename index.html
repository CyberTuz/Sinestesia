How to play

  • Keyboard every letter is a note, numbers play arpeggios, ⌫ makes a glitch
  • Enter the phrase you typed becomes a new groove
  • Mouse left and right opens the filter, up and down adds echo
  • Hold the button to play a tuned theremin
  • Files drop one on the page to hear it
  • Spells hold and draw: triangle fire, circle water, square time, spiral vortex, zigzag lightning
  • Radio drag the dial, scroll, or use the arrows (Shift to jump)
  • Studio build up to eight patterns, chain them into a song, hold the Live FX pads, let Madness vary each bar
  • Tab changes the scene, Esc closes the controls

sinestesia

A techno machine that turns what you type and how you move into music and visions. Build acid grooves in the studio, jam freely, or follow the story.

The Signal is a ten-minute story: seven chapters and an intelligence to put back together, one piece at a time. Best with headphones.

The sound starts quietly and grows on its own. Contains moving lights and intense colours: if you are sensitive to flashing light, turn down “Visions” in the controls.
